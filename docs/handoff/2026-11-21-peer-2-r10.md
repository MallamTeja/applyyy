## Step 1: pacing is not the cause, so no fix is needed

The worker scout run `44886173953` (08:43 UTC):

Pacing and iCIMS together cost 50 s, which is 7% of the run.

**The 03:15 run (before pacing):** 265 boards = gh 104, ashby 73, lever 24, workday 64. Its 64 Workday boards took 28 s in total (0.4 s each, almost all robots deferrals), and its 18 unreached boards were non-Workday.

**So the count fell, not the coverage.**
- Both runs read about 200 non-Workday boards in about 700 s.
- Today's order left the cheap Workday boards after the deadline. They are almost all robots-429 anyway (7 of the 8 reached today), and they go first next run.
- **The ceiling is old, not new:** 283 boards × about 3.4 s is about 16 minutes, more than the 12-minute budget. About one run in two leaves 20–70 boards for the next run, and they rotate. Raising the budget to 16 minutes would cover all of them; that is the orchestrator's call and not needed for this drift.

## Step 3: the liveness sweep skips an unanswered robots.txt, like the Scan

**Change, `supabase/functions/scout-ats/index.ts`, `runLivenessSweep`:**
- The board loop's catch now checks `e instanceof RobotsUnavailableError` first and records the board as `skipped`, with the status, then moves to the next board.
- The close counts `skipped` as neither attempted nor failed.
- A real Disallow, a 403, or a non-robots answer (a Cloudflare HTML challenge) is the parent type and stays `failed`, the same as in the Scan.

**Expected effect:** the 08:46 sweep's 9 "failed" Workday boards drop to those with HTML-challenge answers only. In that run: barclays 503 becomes skipped; blackrock, cisco and dxc (text/html) stay failed, as the Scan does.
