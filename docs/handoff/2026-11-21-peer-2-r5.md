## Design

**D5 is done: one implementation, in a NEW shared file, `_shared/http/request-pacer.ts`.**
- The worker's lane rule, unchanged: the Workday zone (`*.myworkdayjobs.com`) and the iCIMS zone (`*.icims.com`) each get 1 s between request starts. Every other host is undelayed. `PACED_REQUEST_GAP_MS = 1_000`.
- **One deliberate difference:** the pacer *reserves* a slot before it waits. The edge client (`_shared/http/index.ts`) runs requests through a semaphore, so two concurrent callers must not both pass a check-then-set. The worker's pacer assumes a single caller.
- **Worker switch-over (later, peer 5's file):** `worker/src/scout/board-pages.ts` can import `paceLane`, `createRequestPacer` and `PACED_REQUEST_GAP_MS` from `../../supabase/functions/_shared/http/request-pacer.ts`, the same relative-path pattern the worker already uses. It is pure, with no Deno API.

**`_shared/http/transport.ts`:**
- `httpFetch` waits on the pacer before the robots.txt GET and before every attempt, retries included.
- The pacer is on by default: one isolate pacer, like the robots cache. `options.pacer` exists only so tests can inject one.
- A robots cache hit makes no request, so it never waits.

**`scout-ats` (pacing wiring only):**
- New `scout-ats/paced-budget.ts`. It is pure; `index.ts` runs `Deno.serve` on import, so node cannot test it there.
- The Scan loop asks `pacedBoardSkip(provider, last_poll_job_count, adapter.maxPages ?? MAX_BOARD_PAGES, deadline.remainingMs())` before reading a board.
- A paced board that cannot finish before the deadline's 15 s reserve becomes `status: "skipped"` with `paced_budget: needs ~N s, M s left; resumes next Scan`.
- A skipped board gets no write, so `last_polled_at` stays oldest and the oldest-first order puts it first next Scan. Nothing is dropped silently.
- `gate.ts` already counts `skipped` as neither attempted nor failed.
- Unpaced boards are never skipped by this guard, so a Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby Scan behaves exactly as before.

## Seconds per Scan (live numbers, read 13:3x IST)

**Inputs:**
- A Scan reads `DEFAULT_BOARD_LIMIT = 10` boards (max 50), least recently polled first. The handler deadline is `SCOUT_ATS_BUDGET_MS = 100 s`; the platform wall clock is 150 s.
- Today's edge Scans take **76–97 s** (11-20: 97 s, 76 s, 80 s). The ~720 s rows in `agent_runs` are the worker's runs.
- Enabled boards: greenhouse 112, ashby 80, **workday 64**, lever 27, **icims 1** (Atlassian APAC, never polled, so its count is unknown).
- Workday postings: 7 911 across 64 boards, which is 424 list pages at 20 per page: mean 6.6, p90 16, max 20 (the page cap).

**Paced requests per board** = pages + 1 robots.txt GET. The added time is at most 1 s per request: the gap is start-to-start, so a response slower than 1 s eats into it.

| Case | Paced boards in the Scan | Paced requests | Added seconds (upper bound) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typical 10-board Scan (Workday is 64/284 = 22.5% of boards) | ≈ 2.3 Workday | ≈ 2.3 × 7.6 ≈ 17 | **≈ +17 s** |
| One p90 Workday board | 1 | 17 | +17 s |
| One max Workday board | 1 | 21 | +21 s |
| The iCIMS board, first poll (unknown count, so the cap of 60) | 1 | 61 | +61 s |
| Worst 10-board Scan: all Workday at the cap | 10 | 210 | +210 s, **over budget, so the guard caps it** |
| Workday detail reads (`WORKDAY_DETAIL_CAP = 40`) | n/a | ≤ 40 | was a 500 ms pause, now ≥ 1 s: at most +20 s. They already stop at 20 s left (`WORKDAY_DETAIL_MIN_REMAINING_MS`) |

**How the guard keeps this inside the budget:**
- A paced board starts only if `remaining ≥ requests × 1 s + 15 s`. Its paced work therefore ends by deadline − 15 s, which is 85 s of the 100 s.
- The 50 s between the deadline (100 s) and the wall clock (150 s) stays free for response latency and the run-close writes, as before.

**No starvation:**
- The largest single board needs 21 + 15 = 36 s (Workday) or 61 + 15 = 76 s (iCIMS). Both are below 100 s, so every skipped board fits when it comes first in a later Scan.
- Skipped boards keep the oldest `last_polled_at`, so they do come first.

- **Response time is not in the estimate.** The guard budgets the 1 s gaps, not slow responses. Each edge board request has `maxRetries: 0` and the transport's 15 s timeout, and the 50 s past the deadline absorbs that, as it did before this change.
