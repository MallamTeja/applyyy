## Change — `supabase/functions/_shared/screen/tech-gate.ts`

1. **Bare generic title rescue.** A title that is only `Engineer` / `Intern` / `Internship` passes to the matcher, optionally followed by a separator (`- , | : ( /`) and a suffix. The rescue is refused when:
   - the suffix names a discipline: any `TECH_TITLE_SIGNAL` word, or `DISCIPLINE_SUFFIX` (hw, layout, validation, design, process, ops, mba, …);
   - or the title or the first 500 characters of the description name a non-software function (`NON_TECH_FUNCTION`: sales, finance, HR, recruiting, legal, …).

   It runs after the title rules and exclusions, so "Sr Engineer" is never rescued.
2. **DXC entry grade.** On a title the software-role spellings recognise that carries `Sr|Senior Analyst I` (grade I only; `i\b` fails on II, III and IV), only the `Sr` / `Sr.` / `Senior` exclusion terms are set aside. Every other exclusion still applies.
