(secret digests matched). The 503 switch already existed. The bug was that it went one way only: after one primary 503,
every later attempt stayed on the fallback, and the fallback is the weaker model. Peer 2 re-measured live `gemini_usage`
(agent resume-assist, last 7 days): flash-lite 127 ok of 183 attempts, 3.6-flash 2 ok of 35.

**Fix (`client.ts`):** the models alternate. A 503 on the primary switches to the fallback. Any transient failure on the
fallback (503, 5xx, timeout, network) switches back. `maxRetries` still caps the total. `onAttempt` reports "fallback"
only on the way to the fallback; the way back reports "retry". An unset, blank or same-as-primary fallback means no
switch and no throw.

a candidate but has no usage since 11-04, so probe it once before switching.
