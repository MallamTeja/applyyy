### The rule (`jdRemoteLock`)

The rule runs only when both of these hold:
- No part of the board location names any place.
- The policy is `unlocked_or_authorized`. Under `any`, every remote job is kept by definition.

It reads the stripped JD one sentence at a time. A full stop inside "U.S." does not end a sentence. It drops the job on either of two signals:
1. A work word (remote/remotely/work/performed/executed/based/located/reside) followed, in the same sentence, by "anywhere in &lt;places&gt;". The places must resolve to at least one country, none of them authorised, and must not name an open region or one that holds India (worldwide, the world, global, APAC, Asia).
2. A `GMT|UTC ±h to GMT|UTC ±h` window in a sentence that says required/requires/must/only and does not mention "overlap", where IST (+5.5) falls outside the window.

Anything unclear keeps the job, which follows CLAUDE.md §6 and Teja's 11-05 ruling that an unknown location passes. If the board location names an Indian place, the JD lock is ignored.
