## Fix: a server-side top-up, master-only
`topUpNamedSkills` runs only when the turn's kept ops already include an `add_skill`, so removals and questions are never topped up. It reads only the current instruction, never history, so a skill declined earlier is not re-added.
1. **A group is named** when any one of its name's words is said exactly: 3+ letters, not filler, no typo forgiveness. For example, "devops" names "DevOps & Cloud". A whole-name match (AI, Tools) also counts.
   - The group is filled with its master skills not on the resume: the job's `missing` keywords first, then master order.
   - It stops at 6 skills in the group (Teja's 4–6 rule), or takes every skill when the instruction says "all".
   - A second spelling of a skill already present is skipped (`canonicalSkillTerm`).
2. **A master skill is named** in full (`ownerNamed`, with history excluded) or by a 3+ character short form in its parentheses ("hld" names "System Design (LLD/HLD)").
- Every op keeps the master's spelling and group, so `aiEdit.ts:841` auto-applies it (`inMaster`). It is the existing `add_skill` kind, so **no new op kind and no web change**.
- The pass never goes past `MAX_OPS` (12) and respects the `claimed` keys.
