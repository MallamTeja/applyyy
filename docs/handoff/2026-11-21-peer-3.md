## Decisions + evidence

- **The limit was looked up, not guessed.** Source: supabase.com/docs/guides/functions/limits, read 2026-11-21.
  - The request idle timeout is 150 s on every plan, and a late answer gets a 504.
  - The wall clock is 150 s on Free and 400 s on paid plans.
  - The project's org plan is `free` (MCP `get_organization`).
  - scout-ats sends no response until it finishes. So on any plan the gateway answers 504 by about 150 s, and a 180 s client limit can only fire on a dead connection.
  - The test pins `EDGE_CLIENT_TIMEOUT_MS > EDGE_REQUEST_LIMIT_MS`.
- **A race, not an AbortSignal.** The installed functions-js is 2.4.4. Its `FunctionsClient.invoke` builds the fetch init from method, headers and body only, so a `signal` in options would be silently ignored. The race stops the browser's wait. The server may still finish the scan, and the reused `server_timeout` sentence already says so.
- **Opt-in.** No other call site passes the 4th argument, so their behaviour is byte-identical. The test "invokeEdge without timeoutMs waits…" pins this.
- **The wording is reused.** The client timeout reads "Scout-ATS did not answer in time (no answer after 180 s). The scan may still be running…". The 504 text keeps `HTTP 504`, so e2e `/HTTP 504.*Discovered page/` still matches.
- **No new exports from discoveryService.** `remoteDiscoveryRetired.test.ts` pins its export list and the modal's import line from it. The new constants live in edgeCall.ts, and the modal imports them on a separate line.
